## Research Plan: Top Competitor Analysis for AI Solutions & Custom Software & AI Marketing Agencies

This plan outlines the deep research into top competitors across five English-speaking countries, focusing on businesses offering AI solutions and custom software. The goal is to provide your agency with valuable insights for ideation and to analyze effective website strategies.

### 1. Identify Target Countries

I will identify 5 English-speaking countries that meet the following criteria:

*   **Medium Economy:** Countries with a Gross Domestic Product (GDP) between $200 billion and $1 trillion USD (nominal, based on 2023-2024 estimates). This range balances significant market potential with avoiding the most saturated large economies.
*   **Just Starting to Incorporate AI:** Countries showing recent, emerging signs of AI adoption rather than mature, long-established ecosystems. Indicators will include:
    *   Evidence of national AI strategies or significant industry initiatives launched within the last 1-2 years.
    *   Observable growth (over the last 1-2 years) in local AI-focused communities, meetups, AI-related websites, or publicly available data on AI application usage within the country.
    *   Recent news coverage (last 0.5-1 year) highlighting the *initial stages* of AI integration across various business sectors.

After applying these criteria, I will select five specific countries.

### 2. Identify Top Competitors Per Country

For each of the five selected countries, I will identify 20 'top' competitor agencies that offer AI solutions and custom software. This will result in a total of 100 businesses researched. 'Top' will be determined by:

*   **Visibility:** They are among the first to appear in public search results for relevant business types (e.g., "AI solutions agency," "custom AI software development B2B").
*   **Conversion:** Evidence of effective client acquisition, implied by their visibility, professional online presence, and client testimonials/case studies (where visible).
*   **Niche Leadership:** They are recognized as among the best in their specific niche of AI solutions and custom software for B2B clients.

### 3. Conduct Deep Research on Each Competitor

For each of the 100 identified competitors, I will conduct deep research covering the following areas using public data and free tools:

*   **Marketing Strategies:** Overall approach to reaching their target audience.
*   **Advertising (Ads):** Types of ads run, platforms used, and messaging.
*   **Selling Points:** Key differentiators and benefits they highlight to clients.
*   **Products/Services:** Specific AI solutions, custom software development capabilities, and other relevant services offered.
*   **Pricing Models:** How they structure their service costs (e.g., project-based, retainer, hourly rates), if publicly available.
*   **SEO Keywords:** Primary keywords they target to attract organic traffic.
*   **Customer Experience:** Insights into how they interact with clients, from initial contact to project delivery (where observable through testimonials, case studies, or public reviews).
*   **Selling Process:** The general steps a potential client goes through from inquiry to becoming a customer.
*   **Full Website Breakdown:** A comprehensive analysis of their website, detailing:
    *   **Big Importance:** Elements crucial for conversion, trust, and understanding core offerings (e.g., main service descriptions for AI/custom software, clear calls to action, prominent value propositions, detailed client testimonials/case studies, public pricing models, clear navigation to core solutions).
    *   **Medium Importance:** Elements supporting user experience and brand credibility (e.g., high-quality and relevant imagery, detailed 'About Us' and 'Team' pages, relevant blog content on AI/software, case studies without deep details).
    *   **Small Importance:** General stylistic or structural elements that have less direct business impact (e.g., specific font choices not tied to brand strategy, minor decorative design elements, general page layouts unless they directly hinder user experience, standard header/footer elements).
    *   Other elements will include copywriting styles, types of pages and sub-pages, overall website style, and heading structures.

### 4. Deliver Research in Google Docs

All compiled research for each competitor will be organized and uploaded to individual Google Docs, ensuring easy readability and accessibility.

### What Counts

This section clarifies the specific criteria for selecting countries and competitors, based directly on your instructions.

#### Qualifying Countries

*   **Qualifies:** English-speaking countries with a GDP between $200 billion and $1 trillion USD (nominal, 2023-2024 estimates) that show recent (last 1-2 years) signs of emerging AI adoption (e.g., new national AI strategies, recent growth in local AI communities, news discussing initial AI integration).
    *   **Example 1 (Qualifies):** A country with a GDP of $450 billion, a publicly announced national AI strategy in the last 18 months, and a recent increase in AI-focused tech meetups and local AI startups.
    *   **Example 2 (Qualifies):** A country with a GDP of $700 billion, English as an official language, and recent public data showing a significant surge in AI app usage and AI-related academic programs starting in the last year.
*   **Does Not Qualify:** Countries outside the specified GDP range, non-English speaking countries, or countries with either extremely mature AI ecosystems (e.g., established global AI leaders) or virtually no visible AI adoption.
    *   **Example 1 (Does Not Qualify):** The United States (GDP significantly above $1 trillion, very mature AI ecosystem).
    *   **Example 2 (Does Not Qualify):** A country with a GDP of $50 billion (below the medium economy threshold), even if English-speaking and showing nascent AI interest.

#### Qualifying Competitors

*   **Qualifies:** Agencies (not individual freelancers) primarily offering AI solutions and/or custom software development for B2B clients, demonstrating high online visibility, evidence of conversion, and recognized as a leading provider in their specific niche.
    *   **Example 1 (Qualifies):** An agency specializing in developing custom AI automation tools for businesses, ranks on the first page of Google for "B2B AI software development," and features multiple detailed client success stories.
    *   **Example 2 (Qualifies):** A consulting firm that provides AI strategy and implementation services to enterprises, with a strong online presence including paid ads and client testimonials highlighting increased revenue for their clients.
*   **Does Not Qualify:** Freelancers, companies exclusively offering general marketing services without a strong AI/custom software focus, B2C product companies, or businesses lacking significant online visibility or indicators of being a 'top' player in the B2B AI/custom software space.
    *   **Example 1 (Does Not Qualify):** An individual freelancer offering AI-powered copywriting services for small businesses (not an agency, potentially B2C, unclear B2B AI solutions focus).
    *   **Example 2 (Does Not Qualify):** A large corporation selling off-the-shelf AI products to consumers (B2C, not a service agency, not custom software).
