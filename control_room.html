Control room

Pause, resume or stop any running pipeline. Manage your connected APIs and MCP servers, the new tools they bring, and anything that can spend money or do something destructive (delete, send, publish). New tools that only read or change things approve themselves after ten minutes if you don't answer. Money and destructive tools and calls have no time limit: they wait until you answer, and the rest of the pipeline keeps working meanwhile.

Spending limits
A call that would take a service or a pipeline over its limit waits here for you, even when its tool is set to "Always allow". Amounts are in whatever unit the service charges (dollars, credits…), read from the call's own amount or price. A money call with no amount Jarvis can read counts as over any limit you set, so it is always shown to you. Leave a box empty for no limit.
